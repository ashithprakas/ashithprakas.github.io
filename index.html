What the data says about how DTC brands advertise

I wanted to see how direct-to-consumer brands run Google ads. Using a scraper I built and publish on Apify, I collected every ad that Google's Ads Transparency Center listed as shown in the US over 30 days for 50 brand domains. 19 of the 50 returned ads: 4,687 in total.

  • Text dominates. 61% of the ads are text, 22% video and 17% image.
  • Brands differ a lot. 88% of quip's ads and 56% of Oura's are video; 91% of Olipop's and Warby Parker's are text.
  • New ads. 1,008 of the 4,687 (21.5%) were first shown in the last 30 days. Brooklinen started the most (244 of its 722); Gymshark had the highest share of new ads (44%).
  • Churn. The median Gymshark ad had been running 21 days; the median Warby Parker ad, 756.

How it is built

A scraper collects the ads, with retries and rate-limit handling. A script turns them into a clean dataset and a short report. The dashboard sits on top. The same three steps work for your data.
